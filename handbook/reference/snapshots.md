Full current-world snapshots are the initial structural baseline (`NET-003`). The host supplies the initial world and required definitions/appearance assets (`NET-004`). Increasing match-scoped capture numbers govern application: a ready complete 42 can apply after 40 without 41; a late 41 cannot overwrite it (`NET-005`). Current state and validated dependencies determine the result; audio/effects and request delivery have separate policies.

Transport, codec, cadence, dependency delivery, client correction and online reset scope remain open. Review real size/cost/delay early. The [exchange v0.1 bundle](https://github.com/jblick1327/Bomb/tree/gh-pages/handbook/drafts/exchange-v0.1) is a candidate whose schema, types and examples still need reconciliation with Unity; it is not the authoritative requirements source.
