# Verification records

These records describe particular implementations and test targets. Accepted game behaviour is defined by the Architecture.

- [Authoring v4](authoring-v4/summary.md): local environment authoring, destruction and reconstruction.
- [Bounded blast probe](blast-probe.md): blast geometry, recovery and known numerical/performance limits.

Counts, hashes and fixture conventions stay with their dated evidence. They do not establish complete-game or Unity/Odin interoperability. Relevant limits remain linked from the corresponding reference topics.
